MediQueue

MediQueue simplifies hospital visits through appointment booking, live queue tracking, and secure patient verification, helping users spend less time waiting and more time

Overview

MediQueue is a mobile healthcare platform designed to simplify hospital visits by reducing waiting time and improving patient flow management.

The app allows users to book doctor appointments, track live queue status, and complete quick hospital check-ins using QR codes.

Problem

Patients often face long waiting hours at hospitals without any clear information about their appointment status or queue position.

Most hospitals still use manual or unorganized systems, which leads to confusion, overcrowding, and poor patient experience.

Goal

To reduce waiting time and improve patient experience by creating a smooth, transparent, and digital queue management system.

Solution

MediQueue introduces a smart digital healthcare system that simplifies hospital visits and reduces waiting time for patients.

The app provides a seamless experience through online appointment booking, real-time queue tracking, and QR-based fast check-in.

  • Smart Appointment Booking System
  • Live Queue Tracking in Real Time
  • QR-Based Contactless Check-in
  • Doctor Availability Transparency

Key Features

Smart Appointment Booking

Book doctor appointments easily without long queues or manual process.

Live Queue Tracking

Track real-time queue position and reduce unnecessary waiting time.

QR Check-in System

Fast and contactless hospital entry using QR-based check-in.

Doctor Availability

View doctor schedules and availability before booking appointments.
